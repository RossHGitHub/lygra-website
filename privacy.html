Open beta waiting list

How we use your details.

The waiting-list form asks for your name, email address, organisation, role, main playing surface and, optionally, what you would like Lygra to help with.

Why we ask

We use your application to understand who wants to try Lygra, organise beta invitations and contact you about taking part. Joining the waiting list does not create an app account or give immediate access.

Where the application goes

The form sends your details to FormSubmit, which processes the submission and emails it to support@lygra.co.uk. Submitting takes you to FormSubmit’s verification and confirmation pages. Its processing is described in FormSubmit’s privacy policy. The application is received in Lygra’s Gmail inbox.

Your choice

You choose whether to join the waiting list. You can ask us to remove your application or stop contacting you by emailing turfmetricpro@gmail.com.

What this website stores

This static website does not save your form details in browser storage and does not include analytics or advertising scripts. FormSubmit may use anti-spam services on its submission page. Your selected product view can appear in the page URL; it contains no form details.
